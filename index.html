What the history shows

Price wasn't the only problem. It started far too high ($439K, $186/sqft), but at $399K with a $10K incentive (~$169/sqft, right at the similar-home median) and later at $354,900 (~$20K below any similar sale) it still drew no contract.

Relisting made it worse. Four listings, two brokerages, three agents and a 19% price drop. Agents see cumulative days on market, and Zillow shows every cut ? after 9 months buyers assume something is wrong.

Friction kept shifting: occupied vs. vacant; FHA dropped in one listing (cuts out many first-time buyers); no survey in the later listings; thermostat and fixtures withheld; showings by ShowingTime only.

Not visible in MLS: condition, odor, foundation/drainage, the backyard or seller flexibility. ShowingTime keeps buyer-agent feedback from every showing ? the most direct answer to "why."

Lessons for 1216 Lumina Ct

Buyers and appraisers will see 1302's history, and a buyer's agent may cite it ("the one down the street couldn't sell at $355K"). The answer is clear differentiation: AC, sprinklers and water heater all new in 2026, oversized garage, HOA prepaid through 2027, RO system and water softener ? plus a clean, easy showing.

Do the opposite of 1302:
? Price right from the first listing ? avoid starting high and cutting
? Pick one agent and stay with them
? Accept FHA and VA financing
? Have the survey ready
? Leave the thermostat and fixtures
? Keep showings easy ? ideally vacant or staged, with lockbox access
